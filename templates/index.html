Modular End-to-End Biomedical Pipeline

4-Stage Deterministic & Neural Processing
Stage 01 • Ingestion

Signal Acquisition

Ingests continuous single-lead (MLII) ECG voltages from CSV, TXT, or binary streaming files with full format and boundary validation.

Stage 02 • Conditioning

Zero-Phase Preprocessing

Removes respiration baseline wander (<0.5 Hz) and EMG noise (>45 Hz) using Butterworth forward-backward IIR and 60 Hz notch filtering.

Stage 03 • Extraction

QRS Segmentation

Executes Pan-Tompkins R-peak detection, computes estimated Heart Rate (BPM), and slices standardized 360-sample (1.0s) cardiac cycles.

Stage 04 • Deep Learning

1D CNN Classification

Classifies each beat into Normal, SVEB, PVC, or Other Arrhythmia and outputs calibrated confidence probabilities across 4 classes.
