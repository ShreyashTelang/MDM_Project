Screening Observations & Next Steps

  • Normal Sinus Rhythm: Identified uniform P-QRS-T complexes within normal physiological frequency bounds. Continue routine monitoring.
  • Abnormal Ectopic Patterns: Detected morphological deviations consistent with Premature Ventricular Contractions (PVCs) or conduction blocks. A formal 12-lead diagnostic ECG under medical supervision is recommended.
  • Artifact Rejection: Motion artifacts, perspiration, or loose electrodes can generate pseudo-ectopic spikes. Ensure proper lead adhesion.
Reference: ANSI/AAMI EC57 standard for ambulatory ECG analysis.
